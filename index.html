How I work through it

Start rough. Get specific. Build the right version.

  1. 01

    Show me the rough idea

    A sketch, photo, voice-note summary, or “this part, but different” is enough to begin.

  2. 02

    Work out what matters

    Size, material, power, placement, budget, and the parts that absolutely cannot be annoying.

  3. 03

    Pick the right approach

    I narrow down the tools and decisions before adding detail for detail’s sake.

  4. 04

    Finish it so it makes sense

    The result should work, look intentional, and make sense when you use it.

Before we start

The best projects solve a real problem and leave room to find the right answer.

Probably a good fit

You care how it works, how it fits, and how it feels to use. The answer may cross materials or skill sets, and you are open to figuring out the right version together.

Worth talking through first

The timeline is fixed, the design is still moving, or the job depends on equipment, certification, or production volume we have not talked through yet.
