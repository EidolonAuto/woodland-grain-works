From loose thought to real thing

A practical way through the unknowns.

  1. 01

    Show the rough idea

    A sketch, photo, voice-note summary, or “this part, but different” is enough to begin.

  2. 02

    Find the constraints

    Size, material, power, placement, budget, and the parts that absolutely cannot be annoying.

  3. 03

    Choose a build path

    We narrow the tools and decisions before adding detail for detail’s sake.

  4. 04

    Make it understandable

    The result should work, look intentional, and make sense when you use it.

A quick gut check

Good projects start with a real problem and some room to explore.

Probably a good fit

You care how it works and how it feels. The answer may cross materials or disciplines. You are open to figuring out the right version together.

Worth talking through first

The deadline is immovable, the design is not settled, or the request depends on equipment, certification, or production volume not yet discussed.
